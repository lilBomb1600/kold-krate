## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: homeowners researching a cold plunge for their own backyard who want something better made and better looking
than a stock steel or plastic tub, and who are ready to talk to the maker before buying. They arrive on a phone
from Instagram or a shared link, or on a laptop while comparing brands.

Secondary: the owner of Kold Krate himself, who will judge this site as a pitch demo from Arman Digital. It has to
show plainly why it is better than his current site and how it can bring him buyers.

## Product Purpose

Kold Krate builds custom cold plunges, and hot plunges, by hand from California redwood in Santa Cruz, CA. The site
exists to make a visitor want one and to get them to request a quote or call. Success is a filled-in quote request
or a phone call.

## Positioning

Handmade start to finish by one maker in Santa Cruz, from locally sourced California redwood. The claim on his own
site is "the most spacious, most insulated, and most beautiful cold plunge on the market". Each tub is its own
piece because the redwood grain differs. A mass-produced plunge brand cannot truthfully say any of this.

## Operating Context

- Buyers compare against mass-produced plunges (steel, plastic, chest-freezer conversions), so materials, insulation,
  size and build quality are what they weigh.
- He takes inquiries by email and phone. There is no online checkout, and pricing is by quote.
- Instagram: @koldkrate ("Custom built cold/hot plunges handmade with love in Santa Cruz, Ca.").
- His current site, koldkrate.com, is a Lovable template. Its link preview shows Lovable's default image.
